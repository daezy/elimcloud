# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Nigerian SME business owners evaluating cloud ERP and accounting software for their company. They are time-pressed, often non-technical, and highly value trust signals: years in business, local presence, and the range of industries served. They arrive with a specific problem (payroll chaos, multi-branch visibility, inventory leakage) and need to be convinced quickly that Elim understands their industry.

## Product Purpose

Elim Business Ventures Limited's marketing website for two flagship platforms — ECPAS (Cloud Premium Accounting Software) and ECPBES (Cloud Premium Business ERP Software). The site exists to convert visiting SME owners into demo requests and enquiries. Success = a form submission or a call placed.

## Brand Personality

Bold, modern, ambitious. A Lagos-based software company that has been building Africa's business infrastructure for 15+ years — not a startup, not a foreign multinational. Confident without being cold. Local without being small.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS cream/beige landing pages (warm neutrals, pastel accents — the AI-generated default)
- SAP/Oracle enterprise gray (cold, lifeless, government-portal energy)
- Startup-playful (rounded bubbly fonts, confetti illustrations — too casual for B2B enterprise)
- Cluttered / text-heavy (walls of feature bullets, dense spec tables, no visual breathing room)

## Design Principles

1. **Confidence over polish** — Every screen should feel authoritative. Elim has 15+ years and 500+ clients; the design should project that without boasting.
2. **Local roots, global standards** — Nigerian context (Lagos, West Africa, Naira-era business problems) is a feature, not a footnote. Show it proudly.
3. **Clarity at first glance** — SME owners scan; they don't read. Industry verticals, key modules, and the demo CTA must be immediately obvious.
4. **Dark + bold as default** — The current dark-with-pink-accent palette is the brand's strongest asset. Protect it; don't dilute it into beige sections.
5. **Motion earns its place** — GSAP choreography on the hero is justified. Reveal animations on every scroll-triggered element are reflex; audit each one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA as minimum target. Key concerns: contrast on low-opacity white text over dark backgrounds (white/40–50 fails 4.5:1), reduced-motion support for all GSAP animations, keyboard navigability for dropdown menus, and screen-reader labels on icon-only controls.
